Houston · 2026

Texas now lets empty offices become homes. Houston has the offices.

~1 in 4Houston office space sits vacant (25.2%, CBRE, Q4 2025)
Sep 1, 2025Texas SB 840 & SB 2477 take effect: office-to-home conversions by right
65%of the building (and of each occupied floor) must become homes to use the fast-track

What the law changed

In Texas cities over 150,000 people, which includes Houston, an office building at least 5 years old can now become housing without rezoning, a traffic study or extra parking. Houston never had zoning, so the law mostly removes the studies and parking demands that slow a project down.

Why towers still sit empty

Downtown Houston's own conversion study found market forces alone are unlikely to trigger conversions. The blockers are physical and financial: floors too deep for bedroom windows, high retrofit costs, and tenants scattered across floors on long leases, so a tower that is 40% empty can't reach 65% homes.

What Lightwell does

A team of AI agents screens every downtown tower: it tests each floor for daylight, checks the 65% rule, plans the tenant moves into nearby half-empty offices that would unlock a tower, and a red-team agent attacks the plan before you see it.

Sources: Foley: SB 840 & SB 2477 · Texas Legislature: SB 840 · CBRE Houston Office Q4 2025 · Downtown Houston+ / AECOM conversion study. A screening tool, not legal advice.
